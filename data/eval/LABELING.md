# Evaluation labeling rubric

Use `page_now` only when the evidence confirms that a production customer flow, financial obligation, security control, or data-integrity process has halted, corrupted output, or is actively losing work. Use `investigate` for unexplained staleness, abnormal resource or cost movement, partial degradation, or missing output whose production impact is not yet established. Use `defer` for non-production, cosmetic, planned, self-recovered, or maintenance-only events with no current customer or data-integrity impact. Business context resolves superficially similar cases: a missing payroll, tax, settlement, compliance-audit, or customer-balance output is urgent because it breaks a time-sensitive obligation; stale observability in one region, an optional thumbnail surface, or an approved price release that has not propagated is investigated until customer harm or incorrect transactions are confirmed.
